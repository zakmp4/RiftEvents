What makes it scripted

It's an SMP with a writers' room

📜

Written events

Every season is planned out in advance — beats, twists and disasters, scheduled to fire on a date the cast doesn't get told.

🧩

Custom plugins

None of it is vanilla. The rift, the anomalies and the world changes are all bespoke code written for that one moment.

🎭

Real reactions

The events are scripted. The panic isn't. Nobody on the cast is acting, because nobody on the cast knows.

🔗

Lore that carries

Nothing resets between episodes. What breaks in episode two is still broken in episode ten, and it still matters.
