Connecting

What is the server IP?

riftevents.net — Java Edition only.

Which Minecraft version do I need?
Java Edition 1.21.11. Older versions get rejected at the login screen with a version message, so update your launcher profile before joining.
I get "Connection refused" or the server shows offline
Check the IP for typos first, then restart your launcher. If it's still failing, the network may be restarting after a patch — check the Discord announcements channel before opening a ticket.
Can I play on Bedrock or console?
No — Rift Events is Java Edition only. The scripted events rely on custom plugins that have no Bedrock equivalent, so there is no cross-play proxy.

The SMP

Are the events actually scripted?
The events are. The reactions aren't. Every beat of a season is written and coded in advance, but nobody on the cast is told what is coming or when — so what you see them do when a rift opens is genuinely the first time they're dealing with it.
Can I join the SMP?
The cast is fixed for the duration of a season, so mid-season joins only happen when the script calls for one. New spots are announced in Discord between seasons.
Do I need to know the lore to follow along?
It helps. Nothing resets between episodes, so consequences stack up — start at episode one if you can. If you'd rather jump in now, the lore posts on the home page cover what's happened so far without spoiling what's scheduled.
What happens when a cast member dies?
That depends on what the script says that week, and we don't announce the rules in advance. Some deaths cost an inventory. Some cost considerably more.

Ban appeals

How to appeal: open a ticket in Discord with your Minecraft username, the punishment ID from your kick screen, and what you think happened. Appeals are read by a staff member who wasn't involved in the original punishment.
How long does an appeal take?
Usually 24 to 72 hours. Appeals involving screenshare evidence take longer because the recording has to be reviewed in full.
Can I appeal more than once?
One appeal per punishment. A second identical appeal is closed automatically — but you can appeal again after 30 days if you have genuinely new evidence.
What can't be appealed?
Doxxing and hate speech bans are permanent and final. Everything else is open to appeal, including cheating bans.

Reporting players

How do I report someone?
Use /report <player> <reason> in game for anything urgent — it pings online staff instantly. For cheating, a clip is worth far more than a description, so post it in a Discord ticket instead.
Will I be told what happened?
We confirm that a report was actioned, but we don't share the specific punishment. That's for the privacy of the reported player.
What counts as enough evidence?
Unedited video with the player's name visible is ideal. Screenshots work for chat offences. Anything cropped, sped up, or missing context usually can't be actioned.

Account help

I changed my Minecraft username — do I lose my stats?
No. Everything is tied to your UUID, so a name change carries your whitelist access and history across automatically. Mention it in Discord anyway so the Characters page can be updated.
Can I ask for my data to be deleted?
Yes — open a Discord ticket from the account linked to your Minecraft username. Active punishment records are kept regardless, as covered in the privacy policy.
Someone is impersonating me
Open a ticket with both usernames and any screenshots. Impersonation is treated as a moderate offence and handled quickly.
